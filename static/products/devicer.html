Beyond a fingerprint hash

Evidence you can build decisions on.

NEXT turns application-supplied signals into versioned, comparable observations. Know what changed, inspect what contributed, and recognize when the evidence is not enough.

Observe

Version every observation

Keep domain, schema, extractor versions, timestamps, and acquisition provenance together. Trace a result back to the pipeline that produced it.

Compare

See why things match

Declare the relationship you want to test and inspect per-comparator evidence. Missing signals and incompatible configurations produce explicit states, not invented certainty.

Calibrate

Measure on your population

Build calibration artifacts from labeled data, evaluate on held-out samples, and track coverage and abstentions. Raw similarity stays separate from calibrated predictions.

Retrieve

Find exact duplicates

Use content-derived IDs, bounded result sets, deterministic pagination, and integrity-checked snapshots. Exact document matching without pretending to be semantic search.

Govern

Keep history under control

Persist observations in memory, SQLite, PostgreSQL, or Redis. Add authorization hooks, metadata-only audit events, retention purge, and calibration activation or rollback.

Extend

Bring your own signals

Connect typed adapters and application-owned inference. A bounded Node process runtime supports timeouts, cancellation, and teardown without bundling a recognition engine.

NEXT is an engineering preview. Browser change detection and exact document deduplication are the initial advertised workflows; validate each deployment on your own data.

Browser signals

Understand what changed

Compare jointly available browser fields across observations for a declared same-installation relationship. Get a transparent exact-agreement baseline for change review, not an automatic identity verdict.

Document content

Catch repeated content

Identify exact text duplicates in document intake or record processing with content-derived keys. A content match establishes duplication, not authorship. OCR and semantic embeddings are not bundled.

Custom domains

Connect your own extractor

Define input profiles, quality checks, feature dimensions, and extractor versions through the adapter SDK. Biometric adapters are integration contracts, not evaluated biometric products or identity proof.

Two APIs in the Devicer 3.x codebase
Your workflow Existing Devicer API Devicer NEXT preview
Entry point devicer.js devicer.js/next
Compare Weighted device scoring, TLSH, drift analysis Versioned observations, declared relationships, per-comparator evidence
Interpret Heuristic confidence scores and breakdowns Explicit uncertainty, configuration-bound calibration, held-out evaluation
Retrieve DeviceManager matching and LSH candidate lookup Exact content retrieval, deterministic pagination, snapshot integrity
Extend Scoring registries and DeviceManager enrichment plugins Typed domain adapters and injected inference runtimes
Persist Device snapshots through storage adapters Scoped observations, provenance, retention hooks, and rollback

NEXT does not collect signals for you. Normal NEXT JavaScript usage does not require Rust; storage drivers are peer dependencies. High-volume scale and distributed concurrency need separate evaluation.
