How NashTwin Works

Three steps from your current operations to a verified optimal state.

Step 1 — Map

Build Your Digital Twin

Connect your CRM data, org structure, workflows, client accounts, vendor relationships, and pricing models. NashTwin ingests your operational reality and constructs a live, queryable digital twin of your company. Import from existing tools or define your model from scratch.

  • Import from existing CRM exports or connect via API
  • Map teams, roles, clients, vendors, and process flows
  • Define strategic players, payoff functions, and decision constraints
  • Your twin updates in real time as your business changes
Step 2 — Simulate

Run Strategic Scenarios

Run thousands of what-if scenarios in seconds. Change a price, reallocate a team, restructure a vendor contract, or test a new service offering — NashTwin simulates every downstream consequence across your digital twin before you commit to anything in the real world.

  • Multi-agent simulation across all modeled players and stakeholders
  • Scenario branching with side-by-side outcome comparison
  • Pareto frontier analysis to surface non-dominated strategies
  • Coalition analysis for partnership and team composition decisions
Step 3 — Optimize

Converge on Equilibrium

NashTwin's game theory engine solves for the Nash equilibrium of your business — the operating state where no single player, team, or process can improve its outcome by changing strategy unilaterally. That's not just good; it's provably stable.

  • Nash Equilibrium solver for strategic stability across all players
  • Mechanism design tools for aligning incentives across teams and clients
  • Continuous re-optimization as new data flows into the twin
  • Plain-language rationale for every recommended decision
